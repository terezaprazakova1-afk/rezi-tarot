The story

It started with a deck of cards

I've been practising metaphysics since 2005. It began in my teenage years — tarot first, the way most readers start. Then oracle cards, then runes. And then, by chance rather than by plan, an old Slavic method of divination that I've worked with ever since.

Two decades on, I've given thousands of readings — in Prague cafés and boutique spas, at weddings and festivals, over video calls to clients on four continents. I've led lunar workshops and rituals, and I've been booked for events across the Czech Republic, including a session for the cast of an international TV production.

My style hasn't changed since the beginning: precise, grounded, honest. I'd rather tell you a true thing plainly than a pleasant thing beautifully. People book my sessions because they work.

The signature

The old Slavic method

Most of what I know I went looking for. This part found me. Years ago I came across an old Slavic way of divination entirely by chance — I wasn't searching for it, and it wasn't handed to me by anyone. I simply kept working with it, year after year, until it became part of how I read.

In a reading you won't see it announced — you'll feel it. It shapes how I frame a question, how I read timing, and the moments I put the cards down entirely and simply tell you what I see. Alongside it I cast runes and read oracle cards, and the old seasonal wisdom — the moons, the thresholds of the year — runs through my rituals.

It's the quietest part of my practice, and the reason a reading with me doesn't feel like the tarot readings you may have had before.

How I read

Clarity over comfort

I'm a pragmatic reader. I won't dramatise, I won't perform mystery, and I won't promise outcomes — no honest reader guarantees health, money or another person's heart. What I promise instead: you'll leave knowing more clearly where you stand and what your next step is. The cards give the structure; twenty years of practice fills in the detail.

I read in English and Czech, in person in Prague and online worldwide. Every session ends with a photo of your spread, so the reading keeps working after you leave.
