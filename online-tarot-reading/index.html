Before you book

Is the video made just for me?

Yes. I record each reading for one client and their situation. It is not a shared recording for everyone.

What is included for 990 CZK?

An approximately 30-minute personal video. Choose a reading by theme (love, career, wellbeing), by time (till the end of the year, 2027, birthday, lunar cycle) or send your own questions. Inner alchemy is included: exploring your inner patterns and how to work with the reading.

Do we need to be online at the same time?

No. Send your questions and context on WhatsApp, then watch your recording whenever it suits you.

When will I receive my recording?

We confirm delivery before you book. For a time-based reading we also confirm the exact period it covers.

Which languages do you offer?

Personal recordings are available in English and Czech.

Would you rather meet in person?

I offer a limited number of private appointments in central Prague each month. A session costs 1,800 CZK.
