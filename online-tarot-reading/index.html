Questions

Online readings, answered

How do I book an online tarot reading?

Message me on WhatsApp +420 737 079 739 or Instagram. We agree on a time and format, and I confirm everything in writing — payment details come with the confirmation.

What's the difference between live and recorded?

A live reading is a 30–45 minute video call with follow-up questions in the moment. A recorded reading is a personal video made for your questions that you can replay forever. Same price, same depth.

What do I need on my end?

For a live call: a quiet spot and a stable connection — camera optional. For a recording: just your questions.

What about timezones?

I offer slots that work for your timezone, and recorded readings need no scheduling at all.

What if the connection drops?

We reconnect and continue where we left off — you never lose paid time. Worst case, I finish your reading as a recording or we reschedule the remainder.

Which languages?

English and Czech — your choice, live or recorded.
