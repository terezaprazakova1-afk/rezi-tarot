Before we meet

Can I have a tarot reading in English?

Yes. I read in English and Czech. Let me know your preferred language when you book.

Where will we meet?

Sessions take place in central Prague, at Hotel Monchis or an agreed café. We will confirm the exact meeting location when you book.

How much does a private reading cost?

A one-hour private session costs 1,800 CZK. You can pay by QR-code bank transfer or in cash.

Can I book during my visit to Prague?

Send me the dates of your Prague visit on WhatsApp. I offer a limited number of appointments each month and will confirm availability personally.

What if this is my first tarot reading?

You do not need any experience with tarot. Bring specific questions or a situation you would like to explore.

Not in Prague?

You can book a personal video reading for 990 CZK from anywhere.
