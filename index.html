Questions

Before you ask the cards

Do you offer tarot readings in English in Prague?

Yes. I read in fluent English and Czech — in person in central Prague, live on a video call, or as a personal video recording. Booking is via WhatsApp +420 737 079 739; events via e-mail.

Can I book a reading as a tourist visiting Prague?

Absolutely — many of my clients are English-speaking visitors. I keep only a few in-person spots a month, so message me on WhatsApp as early as you can; we meet in the city centre.

How much does a reading cost?

A personal recorded video reading is 990 Kč (about 30 minutes). A private 60-minute session in Prague or by live video call is 1 800 Kč. Tarot for events is from 2 000 Kč per hour, minimum two hours.

How does an online video reading work?

You choose a reading — by theme (love, career, wellbeing) or by time (till the end of the year, 2027, birthday, lunar cycle) — and message me on WhatsApp. I record a private video for you alone and send it within a few days. You can replay it anytime.

What do you read with?

Mariáš cards — the traditional Czech deck — alongside tarot and runes, and an old Slavic method of divination I inherited. Past, present and what's coming, translated into clear, grounded advice. I've read since 2005.

Can you come to our wedding or company event?

Yes — weddings, corporate evenings, festivals and brand activations in Prague and across Czechia, in Czech and English. I set up a quiet reading corner and your guests come one by one. Write to rezitarot@gmail.com with the date.
