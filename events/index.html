How it works at your event

I set up a quiet reading corner at your venue — a small table, two chairs, a candle. Guests drift over one by one for short, personal readings, and drift back to the party glowing. It works at weddings between dinner and dancing, at hen parties as the heart of the evening, and at corporate events as the station people actually queue for.

I read in English and Czech, switching guest by guest — ideal for international weddings in Prague. Booked by the hour, from €80 / 2 000 Kč per hour, minimum 2 hours, in Prague and across the Czech Republic.

Questions

Events, answered

How does hiring a psychic for an event work?

By the hour (from €80/hour, minimum 2 hours). I need a table and two chairs; your guests come for short individual readings throughout the event, in English or Czech.

What kinds of events do you do?

Weddings, hen parties, birthdays, corporate evenings, festivals and boutique events — in Prague and across the Czech Republic.

Event hire or group reading — which do I want?

Event hire (hourly) suits bigger gatherings with guests flowing through. A group reading (per person, minimum 4) is a sit-down session for your own circle.

How far ahead should I book?

Weddings and weekend dates go first — book those as early as you can. For smaller gatherings, message me and I'll tell you honestly what's free.
